💧 What is H2Olivia?

⌄
H2Olivia is a smart water monitoring system that uses sensors to measure water quality and provide real-time data for safer and cleaner water.

📡 How does it work?

⌄
Sensors placed in water collect data such as pH, temperature, and turbidity. The data is processed and shown on a dashboard for easy monitoring.

⏱ Is the data real-time?

⌄
Yes, the system provides live updates for accurate monitoring.

👥 Who can use it?

⌄
Students, households, schools, and communities can use H2Olivia.

✔ Is it easy to use?

⌄
Yes, it is designed with a simple and user-friendly interface.
